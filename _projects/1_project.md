Transcript usage may reveal aging-related patterns that gene expression misses. Under mentorship of Dr. Stephanie Hicks, I developed isoform-level machine-learning workflows for bulk RNA-seq to study age-associated markers in human brain tissue. The analysis represents transcript usage through isoform fractions and combines feature selection with age-prediction models.

Age-prediction features showed limited transfer between hippocampus and dorsolateral prefrontal cortex (dlPFC), suggesting region-specific aging signals. My work included feature construction, model evaluation, comparison of shared and region-specific markers, and visualization of the results. This work was presented as a conference poster at Biology of Genomes, Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory, in 2026 {% cite chen2026bogposter %}.
